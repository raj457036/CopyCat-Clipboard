Background Listening (Android 10+)
CopyCat Clipboard uses the Accessibility Service and Draw Over Other Apps permissions to enable clipboard monitoring in the background.

- Accessibility Service: Required for detecting when you copy text, allowing clipboard content to be saved automatically. CopyCat does not collect or share any data beyond your clipboard content.
- Draw Over Other Apps: Used to create a 0-pixel transparent window that briefly brings the app to the foreground, allowing it to read clipboard content. This is necessary due to Android 10+ restrictions on background clipboard access.

These permissions are only used for clipboard monitoring when enabled and do not impact other app functions.
